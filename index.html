Using the brand

The essentials below come from the DH Network style guide. When in doubt, follow the guide.

Give the logo room

Keep a clear space around the signature. No text, rules, images or other graphic elements may enter it. Wherever possible, use more than the minimum.

Respect minimum sizes

In print, the signature with designation should never be smaller than 34 mm wide; the symbol alone never smaller than 8 mm.

Use approved backgrounds

Place the logo on white, DHN Gray, the secondary palette colours or the faceted pattern backgrounds. On photographs, protect legibility; use the white version on dark or busy images.

Never alter the logo

  • Don’t recolour it or use unapproved colours
  • Don’t remove elements or set it in outline
  • Don’t stretch, distort or rotate it
  • Don’t change the lettering or the proportions between elements
  • Don’t add frames or place elements inside the protection area
